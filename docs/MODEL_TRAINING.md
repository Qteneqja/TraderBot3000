# Model research

The pipeline fits scaled logistic regression by default, with optional explicit LightGBM/XGBoost families. Automated model comparison, Optuna search and deployment remain incomplete.

`train-candidates` uses the same strategy proposals, fixed stop and target as the runtime. Features use completed candles plus the quote available at prediction time. Historical labels use the next open with explicit bid/ask spread, adverse slippage, fees and optional financing; invalidated or drifting entries are excluded. Labels are stop-first, target-first and timeout over 12 bars, conditional on market entry. Same-bar ambiguity becomes stop-first. Incomplete final horizons are omitted. Limit-entry fill labels are not yet trained.

Chronological partitions: 50% training, 15% calibration, 15% validation and 20% final test. Candidate partitions purge every outcome window reaching the next partition; at least five examples of each class are required in fitting and calibration. Scaling fits only training data. Sigmoid calibration fits the held-out segment with a frozen estimator. Do not repeatedly select features/thresholds using final test outcomes.

Candidate reports contain multiclass Brier score, log loss, target reliability buckets, calibration error, split boundaries, data and artifact checksums, and explicit costs. Thresholded candidate economics are labeled overlapping diagnostics, not portfolio returns. Small bucket counts do not establish reliable calibration. Regime stability and uncertainty beyond the assessment's daily block diagnostic remain outstanding.

New candidate versions also include partition sample counts, a training-class-frequency baseline, and a hashed predeclared research policy. The assessment command adds held-out base/stressed portfolio checks and a daily circular-block uncertainty diagnostic; see [MODEL_ACCEPTANCE.md](MODEL_ACCEPTANCE.md). Those diagnostics do not replace independent regime validation or real-time paper evidence.

Artifacts are immutable Joblib files and JSON manifests with checksums and seed 42. Only load trusted local artifacts: Joblib/pickle can execute code. The loader checks the trusted directory, checksum before deserialization, embedded metadata, feature/event schema and outcome classes. Checksums establish integrity, not publisher trust. Held-out replay verifies the original candle count/range/hash; changed or missing historical candles block evaluation. Appended history does not alter the original experiment.

Training produces EXPERIMENTAL registry entries with `execution_validated=false`. The explicit `shadow VERSION` command verifies the artifact and retires a previous shadow model in the same source/instrument/strategy/timeframe scope. The worker records probabilities separately from risk/execution decisions. Predictions before the final calibration outcome are blocked. `replay-shadow` labels modeled historical quotes and cannot call an execution adapter. Database uniqueness prevents duplicate observations per model/candidate. The dashboard distinguishes historical replay from runtime observations.

The older `train` command retains a directional next-open/prior-ATR diagnostic with class-frequency and expanding-window baselines. Its incompatible label schema cannot be shadow-deployed. New candidate models align strategy levels and cost assumptions, and explicitly reviewed versions can enter account-scoped paper trials through a separate operator command. Drift controls, broker promotion and automatic retraining remain incomplete. Verified training used synthetic fixtures only; its metrics are software-test outputs, not market evidence.
